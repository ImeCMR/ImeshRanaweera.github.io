## Professional Summary

I am a PhD student in Chemistry at the University of Florida, reading for my doctorate in Theoretical and Computational Chemistry under the supervision of Prof. Alberto Perez. My research focuses on developing and applying computational methods for drug discovery, with particular interest in machine-learned force fields, molecular dynamics simulations, and protein structure prediction. I have experience with molecular dynamics (GROMACS, AMBER, MELD), machine learning frameworks, and quantum chemistry software (Gaussian, ORCA). My background combines computational chemistry, programming (Python, Fortran, SQL), and data analytics, with a strong foundation in both theoretical and practical aspects of chemical sciences.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Chemistry** — Molecular dynamics, force field development, free energy calculations
- **Drug Discovery** — Structure-based drug design, solubility enhancement, hydrotropic effects
- **Machine Learning** — Neural networks for force fields, property prediction, protein structure prediction
- **Artificial Intelligence** — AI applications in computational chemistry and biology
