# About

Imesh Chamara Madusanka Ranaweera is a PhD student in Chemistry at the University of Florida, specializing in Theoretical and Computational Chemistry. He is a Graduate Research Assistant in the Molecular Recognition Group (Perez Lab), where his research focuses on developing and applying computational methods for drug discovery, including machine-learned force fields, molecular dynamics simulations, and protein structure prediction. He holds a BSc (Hons) in Computational Chemistry from the University of Colombo, Sri Lanka (GPA 3.69/4.00, Second Class Honors – Upper Division).

## Research Experience

### Graduate Level Research Projects

**Beyond Classical Force Fields: Physics-Driven Assessment of the Grappa Machine-Learned Force Field on the FoldBind Dataset** (2026)  
Authors: Imesh Ranaweera, Alberto Perez  
*Investigating the accuracy and transferability of the Grappa machine-learned force field for protein folding and binding using the FoldBind dataset.*

### Undergraduate Research Project

**Investigation of the effects of selected hydrotropes on the solubility of drug-like molecules from Sri Lankan flora: a computational approach** (2022–2023)  
Supervisor: Prof. Samantha Weerasinghe, Department of Chemistry, University of Colombo  
*Studied the hydrotropic effects of pyrogallol and resorcinol on selected drug-like molecules from Sri Lankan flora using molecular dynamics simulations (GROMACS, KBFF20 force field, 300 K, 1 bar). Developed Fortran 95 programs for system preparation, cluster separation, and free-molecule calculations. Found pyrogallol exhibited stronger hydrotropic effects than resorcinol for the selected molecules.*
