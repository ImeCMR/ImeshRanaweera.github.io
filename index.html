Undergraduate Research Project

University of Colombo, Sri Lanka

Investigation of the effects of selected hydrotropes on the solubility of drug-like molecules from Sri Lankan flora: a computational approach

Investigated the hydrotropic behavior of pyrogallol and resorcinol on selected drug-like molecules derived from Sri Lankan flora to characterize solubility-enhancing mechanisms at the molecular level. Performed molecular dynamics simulations using GROMACS with the KBFF20 force field at 300 K and 1 bar, and developed custom Fortran 95 programs for system preparation, cluster separation analysis, and free-molecule calculations. The study showed that pyrogallol exhibited stronger hydrotropic effects than resorcinol for the selected molecules [1].

Supervisor: Prof. Samantha Weerasinghe
